# Knotty visual north star

Adrian picked the **two-ink cutting sheet**, an Overhaul of the Studio built on the world of a 1960s Mexican lumber-yard cut ticket: black press ink and one vermilion spot ink printed on pale straw paper. It replaced the dark-workshop system ([set aside](set-aside/preserve-2026-11/README.md)) after three rounds of generated images and two side-by-side reviews of both systems on the editor, Materials and the site hero. The type was chosen afterwards, in a browser, from real fonts ([type specimen](type.html), option C).

## Visual decisions

- One pale straw paper surface runs under every screen: the header band is solid black ink, and everything below it is paper. There is no dark panel and no white card.
- Structure is drawn with rules, like a printed form. Heavy black rules separate the main regions (header, step strip, stage, panel). Fine rules separate rows inside a panel. Space separates sections before another box is drawn. There are no boxes inside boxes.
- The Studio steps are one horizontal ruled strip of eight numbered cells under the header. Each cell shows its name and its current value. The active step is a solid black cell with light text and a notch pointing down at its open panel.
- The selected thing is black ink, never colour alone. That covers the active step, the chosen option (black fill with a filled radio), the checked box and the part being edited. In the drawing, the edited part is hatched black and tied to its label by a leader line ending in a dot. In Materials, the same hatching marks the piece on the sheet and on the furniture.
- Vermilion appears once per screen, on the primary action («Terminar de editar», «Elegir mi primer mueble»). Nothing else is vermilion, so it never stands for a selection or a warning.
- A warning carries a black triangle mark and its message in sentence case, set apart by a heavy black left bar. It never looks like a selected step or part.
- Furniture is the real natural plywood model in three-quarter view on the paper. Its three dimension lines have arrowheads and horizontal labels. Cut sheets are drawn top-down in plywood tone, with their two dimensions and the waste hatched.
- Type has three roles. **Oswald** (condensed, Alternate Gothic lineage) sets the wordmark, titles, step names and buttons; capitals are used only for step names, panel titles and buttons. **Libre Franklin** sets reading text, help and warnings at 15–18 px. **IBM Plex Mono** sets measurements, quantities, prices and codes. The images were drawn with other faces; [type.html](type.html) is the reference for type.

Still open:
- The strip of eight steps at narrow widths and on mobile, where long values («Triplay de 18 mm · Barniz de poliuretano») already wrap at 1440 px.
- Step transitions and keyboard behaviour.
- Exact colour values. They belong to the brand guide and tokens, not to these images.
- How much hatching and how many heavy rules a long session tolerates. The second review named fatigue as the main remaining risk.

- The Studio image puts vermilion on «Terminar de editar», which leaves the editor rather than doing the screen's work. Revisit when the Studio is built against this image.

## Materials on desktop and the take-away guide

Both extend the system above; neither adds a token, a face or a second vermilion.

- **Materials on desktop, verdict first.** Under the furniture band and «Preparar materiales», one wide band in two halves: on the left the sideboard large, with the hatched «Techo» and its leader, and the cost under it as a secondary line with the price qualifier; on the right «Se puede hacer», then the pending decision with its heavy left bar and the wall options, and the one vermilion action directly under it. Below, a full-width ruled band with «Comprobaciones de Knotty», its six checks and «Repetir revisión» in ink. Then «Hojas de triplay»: the material strip and the sheets as evidence for the verdict above, at a smaller scale. The furniture stays on screen; the phone keeps today's one column.
- **Take-away guide, a route of phases.** Under the document masthead and the pending-review notice, one ruled strip of five numbered cells under two black role headers: «Para la maderería» over 01 «Lista de corte» and 02 «Mecanizado y perfiles», «Para armar» over 03 «Compra de herrajes y consumibles», 04 «Armado por fases» and 05 «Ajustes e instalación». A heavier rule marks the handoff between 02 and 03. Each section below carries its role as a black tag at its left, so a printed page says whose it is without the strip. The vermilion print button is screen-only.

Known defects of these images, not to be built:
- The guide pins the «Herramienta: …» lines to phases 1–3. In the guide they belong to the joints (pocket screw, screw, nail), not to a phase.
- The guide squeezes phases 4–9 into narrow cells, and its sideboard overlaps the masthead band. Phases are rows with room for their steps.
- On Materials' sheet 1, «1. Techo» is drawn over «1. Piso», and sheet 2 has no piece labels. The sheets are drawn by the app's own diagram, labels included.
- «Sobra al menos 396 × 130 mm» floats beside sheet 2; it belongs to its sheet's caption, as the app prints it.

## Site and catalog

The public site is a late-1960s lumber-yard brochure in the same inks, paper and type: the brochure register shows, the ticket register is used. Every page shares one masthead: a black band with an empty square before «Knotty» (the knot mark goes there), ink links, and one outlined ink button at the right.

- **Landing, the shop window.** An asymmetric spread: the hero and plate 01 (the sideboard with its three dimension lines and a ruled caption «Aparador con patas | Mueble de ejemplo | KC-APA-01») on the left, an index of three bases as numbered ruled rows on the right. Vermilion is only «Elegir mi primer mueble»; «Explorar el catálogo» is ink. On a phone it is one column, with plate 01 starting above the fold.
- **Landing, materials section.** It shows the product's output at a readable size: a black leader line runs from the sideboard's top to the same piece, «Techo», on a cut sheet drawn top-down, with the waste hatched. «Ajustar el aparador» is ink, because it is not the page's primary action. The three steps above it are one ruled strip.
- **Brand guide** (`/marca/`), the brochure's colophon: a ruled section nav, logo plates, swatches as ruled cells and the three type specimens. The plates were left empty on purpose; the logo is drawn from the vector.
- **Catalog home, rooms as chapters.** One ruled row of room cells with counts, the selected room a solid ink cell; the count line follows it. Furniture are numbered plates of plywood line drawings with a ruled caption (name in condensed bold, style in the reading face, measures in mono). No hatching, because hatching means «the selected part»; no vermilion, because the screen's action is opening a plate.
- Furniture is drawn two ways, and both are the app's own: the Studio's 3D render where the site sells one piece, the catalog's line drawings where it lists many.

Known defects of the images, not to be built:
- The landing's height line stops at the bottom of the body; it runs from the floor to the top (940 mm includes the legs).
- The catalog's TV unit has angled 1960s legs. No piece in the catalog has them; draw each piece from its own model.
- The cut sheet's proportions and its «desperdicio 9 %» are illustrative. The built section shows what the app computes for the sheet.
