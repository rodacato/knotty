# Image Gen prompt

```text
Create “El aparador armado,” one realistic, production-quality desktop website hero screenshot for Knotty, a Mexican product that helps someone choose a plywood furniture base, adjust its dimensions, and understand how to build it. Output a single crisp 1440 × 1024 px landscape image, exactly the 45:32 viewport aspect ratio. Flat and edge to edge, with no browser chrome, device frame, outer background, paper-object mockup, perspective on the page or content below the hero. All requested words, the cabinet, the three dimension strings, and the figure footer fit fully inside this desktop frame with breathing room.

Four attached references in order:
• PNG 1: mandatory reference for Knotty’s shared visual language: nearly black ink, compressed bold title typography, mono data typography, stark rules and vermilion spot color. Use the same typographic “Knotty” treatment in a restrained black masthead, but replace its saturated yellow with the much paler stock specified below.
• PNG 2: mandatory reference for the graphic logic of the two-ink technical sheet, strong ink lines and alignment. Do not copy its yellow, red selection, tables or Studio content.
• PNG 3: direct composition and content reference for the public home page. Keep its asymmetric editorial arrangement, its own navigation and the large finished cabinet drawing with measurements. Revise paper, typography and spacing as specified below. Do not copy any Studio interface, tabs, panels, mode labels, edit actions or colored selection on the cabinet.
• JPG 4: geometry and material of the cabinet only. Ignore its interface, palette outside the wood, grid, object labels and exploded pose. Reassemble the furniture completely in natural golden pine plywood with a layered edge and straight 150 mm legs. Show an exterior three-quarter view with the top and right side visible, at its true wide proportions: 1600 wide × 940 high × 400 deep. Preserve the exact cabinet family: three grooved inset doors below the upper band, one small upper-left drawer, three open upper niches, and two stacked lower-right drawers. Keep the joinery and front layout plausible and do not add handles, props, room furniture or new styling.

ART DIRECTION — Hoja de despiece de una maderería mexicana, a graphic counter ticket printed in two inks on very pale straw manila. Use a uniform matte pale straw field, around #F8F2DF, almost cream, neither white nor the saturated yellow in the source images. Nearly black press ink for typography, squared rules and active states. Use Barlow Condensed bold (upright, condensed and slightly industrial, as on forms printed at a wood supplier) only for titles, strong labels and the step strip. Set every paragraph, help, notice and ordinary UI label in IBM Plex Sans, 15–16 px with ample line height for prose; use IBM Plex Mono only for measurements, quantities, prices and codes. Heavy black rules only where needed to establish the technical sheet, with fine column separators and the graphic rhythm of a tear-off cut sheet. Group by spacing and alignment first, then a single rule. No boxes inside boxes. The cream paper, dimensional drawing, ruled cells, angled technical leader and diagonal drafting hatch should read instantly as a modern lumberyard cutting sheet, not a generic beige app or a retro poster.

The specific craft signature is the juxtaposition of sharply typeset workshop measurements and the beautifully assembled plywood piece, on a pale-straw, ink-ruled field. Reserve vermilion exclusively for the principal button, “Elegir mi primer mueble”. Make the button square, bold, high contrast, and outlined in black so that its affordance does not rely on color. Nothing else is vermilion.

Composition: a solid near-black masthead across the top with condensed warm-cream “Knotty” at the left as typeset text, and two generously spaced, right-aligned navigation links. Below the header, use one continuous pale straw surface across the whole hero. Design an asymmetrical editorial two-column spread with a long heavy horizontal black registration rule and one fine column divider. Keep the copy block on the left at around 38–40 percent of the width, the still life and measurement drawing on the right at around 60–62 percent. Position the eyebrow directly above a deliberately stacked Barlow Condensed headline at a bold editorial scale, with natural phrase breaks and balanced rag. Align the warm conversational paragraph, vermilion CTA and supporting note beneath it. The paragraph and note are IBM Plex Sans regular at 15–16 px with comfortable line spacing; do not render them in mono or condensed type. Use easy 44 px or larger target sizes for both nav links and CTA. No cards and no visual clutter.

The hero cabinet must be the standout image on the right: large, fully visible and recognizably built, with the quiet sheen, detailed laminated edges and natural grain of the reference furniture, in the same kind of isolated warm 3D render as the first attachment. The pale straw field continues behind it, without a room, photograph, horizon, backdrop frame, selection or artificial scenery. Give the three black dimension callouts bold and equally legible mono type, clean arrowheads and drafting extension lines. Associate “940 mm · 94 cm” with the vertical height line from top to feet; allow enough room for the whole line and label. Associate “1600 mm · 160 cm” with the full width of the cabinet. Associate “400 mm · 40 cm” with its depth, clearly on the diagonal depth axis. Set all three annotations in spaces clear of the cabinet and each other. The dimension arrows follow the object axes while label baselines remain horizontal. Put a strong ruled figure footer directly beneath the drawing. Place “Aparador con patas” as the dominant footer label, “Mueble de ejemplo” as a smaller secondary label, and “KC-APA-01” as a compact figure code. The footer belongs to the drawing.

Complete verbatim visible copy in Mexican Spanish, with exact spelling, accents, punctuation, casing and numbers. There are no other strings, badges, prompts, menu items, headings or CTAs:
Navigation: “Knotty” / “Cómo funciona” / “Muebles”
Eyebrow: “Muebles de triplay · Hechos por ti”
Headline: “Ese rincón ya tiene su próximo mueble.”
Body: “Elige una base, ajústala a tus medidas y entiende cómo se arma. Knotty te acompaña del diseño 3D a las piezas que llevas al taller.”
Primary button: “Elegir mi primer mueble”
Note: “Puedes empezar con las bases sin conectar un experto.”
Height: “940 mm · 94 cm”
Width: “1600 mm · 160 cm”
Depth: “400 mm · 40 cm”
Figure footer: “Aparador con patas” / “Mueble de ejemplo” / “KC-APA-01”

Avoid purple-to-blue or indigo gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, uniform rounded cards with soft shadows, nested cards, decorative emoji or default icon sets, centered hero plus three feature cards, neon-on-black, gradient buttons, dummy text, invented numbers and evenly repeated sections. No invented people, marks, logos, stamps, QR codes or photographs. No old, torn, creased, folded, scratched or distressed paper. Use only the explicitly requested copy, sharp and legible, preserving Spanish accents, figures and punctuation.

The result is a working modern website hero in the same specific two-ink world, with an unmistakable call to choose a first furniture base.
```
