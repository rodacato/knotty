## A bookcase

A bookcase («librero» or «estante») is an open cabinet that holds books on shelves.

- Open shelves with books call for a back panel and a fixed or adjustable shelf row.
- Keep the shelves close enough that a book does not leave a gap above it.

# short

A bookcase holds books on open shelves.
Keep the shelves close enough that a book does not leave a gap above it.
