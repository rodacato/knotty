# 1
Tools: the person has a drill, a countersink, a square and a few clamps, and gets the boards cut at the store. Do not prescribe a circular saw, pocket jig, table saw or router: offer a joint they can make with a drill, or ask the store to cut, drill or groove for them.

# 2
Tools: the person also has a circular saw with a guide, a pocket jig and a hinge-cup bit. Do not prescribe a table saw or router: for what needs them, offer another joint or a store cut service.

# 3
Tools: a full workshop with table saw, router and long clamps. Do not assume a particular bit or jig beyond that, and do not make the person buy more.
